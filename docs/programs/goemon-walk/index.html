Goemon — Audio + Branching Build

This version combines the three next steps: a stronger game-like exploration build, sound cue encounters, and branching routes. The walk is faster, the route is shorter and denser with things to notice, and the branches hide events until you round corners.

Design of the test: run the Baseline Route first to get a normal walking trace. Then run the Kabuki Route with shamisen, flute, gong, Kabuki yell, sound-only trigger zones, stationary apparitions, dual actor/fox moments, and a bridge-over-water path.

Controls: W/S move forward/back, A/D shift laterally, mouse or on-screen turn buttons to look. At forks, choose with 1/2 or Left/Right.
