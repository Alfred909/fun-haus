What counts as a stop?

A guest pauses at the viewing area and faces Goemon for at least 3 simulated seconds. Each guest is counted once.

How guests decide

Each guest receives seeded curiosity, urgency, energy, hunger and social values. Visibility affects noticing. Curiosity and energy encourage stopping; urgency, hunger and crowding discourage it. Nearby watchers can attract attention.

These are illustrative rules, not calibrated human predictions. Agents move independently; this version does not model physical collisions.

The same seed repeats a guest population. CSV includes probabilities, needs, settings and each decision.
