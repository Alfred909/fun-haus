Direct Matter Manipulation Simulator

A planned experiment in shaping simulated material directly, then examining how the interaction changes its behavior.

The simulator is in concept development. These modes and measurements describe intended work; a playable model and experimental results are not available yet.

Two planned ways to explore

Interactive mode

A hands-on sandbox for selecting, moving and reshaping simulated matter. Guided challenges would make cause and effect visible through immediate feedback, with pause, reset and undo controls for exploration.

Scientific mode

A controlled workspace for documenting material assumptions, changing one condition at a time, and comparing repeatable trials. Planned tools include parameter controls, a fixed seed where randomness is used, a time-series view and data export.

Research overview

The proposed question is how direct manipulation and visible feedback affect a person's ability to predict, control and understand a modeled material system. A future study could compare free exploration with guided tasks and controlled parameter changes.

Development would begin with a documented material model, explicit units and boundary conditions, followed by numerical checks and comparison with suitable reference cases. User studies would examine task performance and understanding separately from model accuracy.

The detailed model, literature selection and study protocol remain to be established. This overview is a provisional exhibit scope, not a report of completed research or evidence of a physical matter-manipulation device.

Intended metrics

Proposed measurements; definitions, units and acceptance criteria will be set with the model and study design.
MeasureWhat it would capture
Task completionSuccess rate, completion time, actions and corrections for a defined task.
Control accuracyDistance or shape error relative to a target, in units appropriate to the model.
Learning and understandingPrediction accuracy before and after exploration, perceived control and workload.
Material responseModel-dependent displacement, deformation, force or energy over time, where supported by the solver.
Numerical reliabilityRepeatability, sensitivity to time step and resolution, and conservation error where required by the model.
Interactive performanceInput-to-feedback latency, frame time and simulation steps per second.
No launch date is set. Planned measurements are not collected results. Check back in the Simulation Lab as the prototype develops.
