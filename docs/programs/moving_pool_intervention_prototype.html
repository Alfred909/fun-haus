Moving Pool Intervention Prototype

Same guest behavior as the original: if a family wants tubes, they keep collecting until they have all the tubes they want. Seeing other families collect tubes makes later families more likely to do the same. The design change is that tube collection happens inside a broad moving pool instead of blocking the narrow river entry.
