02 / THEMED LEARNING & PARTICIPATION

Roman Soldiers

A connected Cub Scouts experience

I combined requirements from multiple projects and badges into one Roman Soldiers storyline. Scouts earned citizenship, developed skills, and prepared for a culminating event called the Siege.

I designed the activity sequence and safety rules, connecting the story to first aid, hiking, construction, teamwork, and play.

  1. 01

    Earn citizenship

    Establish a shared identity and prove skills through Scout requirements.

  2. 02

    Build and prepare

    Create a banner, boffer swords, shields, and catapults that launch marshmallows at targets.

  3. 03

    Apply the learning

    Find volunteers portraying injured soldiers on a survivor hike and use practiced first-aid skills.

  4. 04

    Take part in the Siege

    Bring the narrative together through games and battle play structured by safety rules.
