Fun Haus v08 — Lab programs / Office information

Open FunHaus-v08-standalone.html, or extract the ZIP and open funhaus/index.html.
Three.js and all PRGE photos are included. No server or internet is required.
Desktop browser with WebGL and pointer lock recommended.

WASD walk / mouse look / Shift sprint / E nearby information / Esc release.
Aim at a paper on the board or a wall screen; left-click to open the subject.

LAB
Enter the blue-trim room. Its PRGE 2019 screen opens a project preview.
Click Yes, play to launch the actual recovered documentary program from the
Beyond the Building data-analysis website. It has a 164-second moving model,
17 original event photographs, play/pause, speed, seeking and scene controls.
Click a photograph to enlarge it. Esc closes an enlarged photo; otherwise Esc
exits the program. The Exit to lab button is always outside the program and
unloads it immediately, stops its animation, and recaptures the mouse.
Your exact location and viewing direction are preserved. Esc exits to a paused
room; click Resume to recapture the mouse.

The documentary runs in a separate sandboxed frame. It begins loading only
after confirmation and is removed on exit. No autoplay occurs in the preview.
The dots, paths, counts and timings are illustrative, not measured event data.
The research project's original source files were not modified.

OFFICE
The terracotta room holds a project/research information register.

An independent printable copy is also provided as resume.html.
